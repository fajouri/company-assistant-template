# Remote work

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week, subject to manager approval.

Remote work arrangements must not affect the employee's availability for scheduled meetings.

## Equipment

The company provides a laptop and a standard home-office allowance.

Employees are responsible for a safe and ergonomic home workspace.

## Availability

Remote employees must remain reachable during core working hours.

Core working hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in the company's primary time zone.
