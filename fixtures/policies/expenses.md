# Expenses

Employees can claim reasonable business expenses with original receipts.

Claims must be submitted within 30 days of the expense date.

## Travel

Rail and standard-class air travel are reimbursed at cost.

Mileage for personal vehicles is reimbursed at the published rate per kilometre.

## Limits

Single expenses above 500 must be approved in advance by a manager.

Entertainment expenses require director approval.
