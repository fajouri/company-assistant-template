# Annual leave

Every full-time employee accrues 25 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.

Part-time employees accrue leave on a pro-rata basis, rounded up to the nearest half day.

## Requesting leave

Submit a request through the internal portal at least five working days before the first day of leave.

Managers confirm or decline requests within three working days.

## Carry-over

Up to five unused days may be carried into the next calendar year.

Carried days expire on 31 March of the following year.

# Public holidays

Public holidays are not deducted from the annual leave balance.
