# Health insurance

The company contributes to a group health plan for all eligible employees.

Enrolment opens during the first month of employment and annually during open enrolment.

## Eligibility

Employees working at least 60 percent of full time are eligible.

Dependants can be added during open enrolment or after a qualifying life event.

# Pension

The company matches employee pension contributions up to 5 percent of base salary.

Matching starts after three months of continuous service.
