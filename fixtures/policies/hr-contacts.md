# HR contacts

This document lists internal escalation contacts for the HR team.

It is restricted to HR staff and must not be shared outside the team.

## Escalation

For payroll questions, contact the Payroll Lead.

For absence and leave disputes, contact the Employee Relations Lead.

## Confidentiality

All information in this document is confidential.

Access is logged and reviewed quarterly by the HR Director.
